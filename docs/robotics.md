# Robotics
I like leading robotics projects!

Throughout the 2025-2026 school year I was a software member and eventually the software lead of robotics team at Space Concordia. 
For that year our goal was to compete at the University Rover Challenge (URC).
As a team we worked extremely hard to get the rover fucntional for the competition.
We pulled through and made it to URC 2026!
As the software lead I made sure that the base station and rover were ready to compete in the missions.
This was an amazing experience and I want to go again.
I currently am putting my efforts towards leading the system engineering of the robotics team. 
This year we have to goal of competing at URC 2027 and the European Rover Challenge as well.
